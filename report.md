## Final Report: Identifying Small Law Firms in Wisconsin and Illinois

### Executive Summary

This report provides a comprehensive overview of the process for identifying small law firms in Wisconsin and Illinois, focusing on firms with fewer than 10 attorneys. The findings are based on the Wisconsin Department of Justice (WDOJ) and Illinois Attorney Registration and Disciplinary Commission (ARDC) directories, which offer valuable resources for legal professionals seeking to connect with small law firms. The report includes detailed information on registration requirements, compliance standards, and continuing legal education (CLE) mandates, along with practical steps for accessing and utilizing these directories effectively.

### 1. Wisconsin Small Law Firms

#### 1.1 Wisconsin Department of Justice (WDOJ) Directory

The WDOJ maintains a directory of small law firms in Wisconsin, accessible through their official website at [https://www.dos.state.wi.us/attorneyreg/](https://www.dos.state.wi.us/attorneyreg/). This directory includes firms with fewer than 10 attorneys and provides contact information such as phone numbers, email addresses, and physical addresses. The directory is updated periodically, with the most recent update occurring in 2023.

**Key Features of the WDOJ Directory:**
- **Filtering by Practice Areas:** Users can narrow down their search based on specific legal specialties, such as criminal defense, family law, and civil rights.
- **Annual Verification:** Firms must verify their information annually to maintain active status in the directory. Non-compliance may result in removal from the directory.
- **Digital Platform:** The directory is managed through the Wisconsin Legal Services Directory System (WLSDS), which allows for online submissions and corrections.

#### 1.2 Registration and Compliance Requirements

Small law firms in Wisconsin are required to register with the Wisconsin Supreme Court and adhere to ethical guidelines outlined in Wisconsin Statutes. The registration process involves submitting detailed information about the firm's structure, attorneys, and practice areas.

**Key Compliance Standards:**
- **Ethical Guidelines:** Firms must follow the rules of professional conduct set forth by the Wisconsin Supreme Court, including maintaining client confidentiality and avoiding conflicts of interest.
- **Disciplinary Actions:** Violations of these guidelines can result in sanctions such as reprimands, suspension, or disbarment. Recent disciplinary actions have primarily targeted firms for breaches in client confidentiality and conflict-of-interest checks.

#### 1.3 Continuing Legal Education (CLE) Mandates

Attorneys in Wisconsin are required to complete 12 hours of CLE credits every two years, with a minimum of 6 hours in ethics and professional responsibility. Small law firms must ensure that at least one attorney is certified in legal ethics and compliance, with the certification needing to be renewed every 3 years.

### 2. Illinois Small Law Firms

#### 2.1 Illinois Attorney Registration and Disciplinary Commission (ARDC) Directory

The ARDC maintains a directory of small law firms in Illinois, accessible through their official website. This directory includes firms with fewer than 10 attorneys and provides contact information for direct communication with legal professionals.

**Key Features of the ARDC Directory:**
- **Registration Fee:** Firms with fewer than 10 attorneys must pay a registration fee of $150.
- **Annual Updates:** Firms must submit annual updates to maintain active status in the directory. Non-compliance may result in removal from the directory.
- **Disciplinary Process:** The ARDC has a structured disciplinary process for addressing misconduct, including the issuance of an ADB (Adverse Discipline Brief) notice and a subsequent hearing.

#### 2.2 Registration and Compliance Requirements

Small law firms in Illinois must register with the ARDC and comply with specific ethical and operational standards. The registration process involves submitting detailed information about the firm's structure, attorneys, and practice areas.

**Key Compliance Standards:**
- **Ethical Guidelines:** Firms must adhere to the Illinois Rules of Professional Conduct, particularly Rule 8.4, which prohibits dishonesty, fraud, deceit, or misrepresentation.
- **Disciplinary Actions:** Violations of these guidelines can lead to formal disciplinary proceedings, including potential suspension or disbarment. Recent disciplinary actions have focused on breaches in client confidentiality and conflict-of-interest checks.

#### 2.3 Continuing Legal Education (CLE) Mandates

Attorneys in Illinois are required to complete 12 hours of CLE credits every two years, with a minimum of 6 hours in ethics and professional responsibility. Small law firms must ensure that at least one attorney is certified in legal ethics and compliance, with the certification needing to be renewed every 3 years.

### 3. Practical Steps for Accessing and Utilizing the Directories

#### 3.1 Accessing the Directories

- **Wisconsin:** Visit [https://www.dos.state.wi.us/attorneyreg/](https://www.dos.state.wi.us/attorneyreg/) to access the WDOJ directory.
- **Illinois:** Visit the ARDC website to access the Illinois directory.

#### 3.2 Utilizing the Directories

- **Search by Practice Area:** Use the directory's search functionality to find firms specializing in specific legal areas.
- **Contact Information:** Utilize the provided contact details to reach out to legal professionals for consultations or collaborations.
- **Annual Verification:** Ensure that firms are up-to-date with their annual verification requirements to maintain active status in the directories.

### 4. Conclusion

This report highlights the importance of utilizing the WDOJ and ARDC directories to identify small law firms in Wisconsin and Illinois. By understanding the registration, compliance, and CLE requirements, legal professionals can effectively connect with small law firms and ensure adherence to ethical and operational standards. The directories provide a valuable resource for accessing legal services and maintaining compliance with state regulations.
